Accessibility

Our commitment to accessible digital experiences.

Version 1.0 Last Updated: August 2026

Our Commitment

Talent Bridge BD is committed to supporting accessible digital experiences and improving the usability of its public-facing technology platforms.

Accessibility Practices

TBBD aims to use accessible page structures, readable content, meaningful navigation, descriptive alternative text, keyboard-friendly interactions, and responsive layouts where applicable.

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. TBBD may review and improve digital experiences as technologies, standards, and user needs evolve.

Feedback

Users who encounter accessibility barriers are encouraged to contact TBBD through its official business channels with details that can help identify and address the issue.
