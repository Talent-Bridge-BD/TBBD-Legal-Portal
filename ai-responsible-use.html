Responsible AI

Principles for responsible use of AI-enabled TBBD services.

Version 1.0 Last Updated: August 2026

Human Oversight

AI-enabled services are intended to assist people and should be used with appropriate human review and judgment.

Accuracy and Verification

AI-generated information may contain errors or omissions. Users should verify important information before relying on it for consequential decisions.

Privacy and Security

Users should avoid submitting confidential, sensitive, or unnecessary personal information to AI services unless the applicable service specifically permits such use and appropriate safeguards are in place.

Fairness and Responsible Use

AI-enabled services should be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable policies, agreements, and legal requirements. AI should not be used to facilitate unlawful discrimination, harmful activity, or unauthorized access to information.

Employment and Business Decisions

Where AI-enabled services support recruitment, HR, or business processes, appropriate human oversight and applicable organizational policies should remain part of the decision-making process.

Continuous Improvement

TBBD continuously evaluates its AI-enabled services and technology practices to improve reliability, security, transparency, and responsible use.
